# The decision, as a sentence

[#n](https://github.com/OWNER/REPO/issues/n) decided on DATE, on EVIDENCE (a trial, a measurement, a pilot result), that DECISION, because:

- **Consideration.** What was found or tried, and what it ruled out.
- **Consideration.** The alternative, and why it lost.

What follows from it: what the code does now, what a later change must keep, and what was deliberately not done.
